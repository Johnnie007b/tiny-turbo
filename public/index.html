      HOW TO PLAY

      COMPUTER

      Arrows / WASD steer, gas, brake
      Space / Shift drift (handbrake)
      Esc pause
      Gamepad: stick steer, RT/A gas, LT/B brake, X drift

      PHONE / TABLET

      Hold GAS and use ◀ ▶ to steer (or switch to slide-steer in Settings). Tap DRIFT in corners. Turn your phone sideways!

      MODES

      RACE: classic laps. First across the line wins.
      DUEL: stay close to the leader! Fall too far behind and you're out. Last car racing scores a point.
